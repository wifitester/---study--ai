Master Problem-Solving Protocol

Use this universal 5-step framework to solve any Physics 12 problem without missing marks.

1. Coordinate & System Setup

Establish positive/negative axes. For inclines, rotate the x-axis parallel to the ramp surface.

2. Isolate & Draw Free-Body Diagrams (FBD)

Represent forces as arrows originating from the center of mass. Never include components on the primary FBD.

3. Vector Component Resolution

Decompose off-axis vectors: $F_x = F \cos\theta$ and $F_y = F \sin\theta$. Verify vector signs (+/-).

4. Independent Equations ($\Sigma F_x = ma_x$, $\Sigma F_y = ma_y$)

Write separate force or momentum balance equations for vertical and horizontal axes.

5. Unit & Sanity Check

Verify sig-figs, units ($N, J, kg \cdot m/s$), and ensure velocity doesn't exceed speed of light!

High-Frequency Exam Traps

  • Assuming $F_N = mg$: On inclines, $F_N = mg\cos\theta$. If pulling at an upward angle, $F_N = mg - F_a \sin\theta$.
  • Scalar vs Vector Addition: Energy is scalar (add directly). Momentum is a vector (MUST add using x/y components or vector triangles!).
  • Orbital Radius Offset: $r$ in $F_g = G \frac{M m}{r^2}$ is measured from the center of the mass ($r = R_{planet} + \text{altitude}$).
